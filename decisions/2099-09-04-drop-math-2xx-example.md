# Drop MATH 2XX-EXAMPLE in fall 2099

## Question

Keep all 16 credits in fall 2099, or drop MATH 2XX-EXAMPLE while a drop leaves no transcript notation (through Fri 2099-09-11)? [Source: example.edu academic calendar, read 2099-09-01]

## Options considered

| Option | For | Against | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Keep 16 credits | math requirement done on schedule | ~12 h/week of problem sets; a 40% final in an already full finals week | [[school/courses/math-2xx-example]] syllabus §Grading |
| Drop to 13 credits | time for DEMO 101 (4 cr) and internship applications | the 200-level math requirement moves to a later term | [[school/academic-profile]] degree audit |
| Keep it, drop SAMPLE 120 instead | math stays on schedule | the studio is the only breadth course with open seats this term | registrar listing, 2099-09-02 |

## Decision

**Dropped MATH 2XX-EXAMPLE on Fri 2099-09-04.** 13 credits remain, still above the full-time minimum of 12.

## Why

- The first two problem sets took about 12 hours each; that time is needed for internship applications this fall. [Source: Alex, 2099-09-04]
- The course offers an online section in spring, so the requirement can move without delaying graduation, per the advisor. [Source: Dr. Demo Advisor, email 2099-09-03]

## What this gives up

- One more requirement to fit into the remaining terms, which narrows spring options (see [[school/2100-spring-study-abroad-exploration]]).

## Revisit if

- Spring 2100 planning shows the math requirement no longer fits before May 2101. Check at registration (2099-11-17).
