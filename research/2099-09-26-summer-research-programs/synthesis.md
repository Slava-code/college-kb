# Summer 2100: research programs vs internships — synthesis (2099-09-26)

**Question:** Should Alex apply to undergraduate summer research programs for summer 2100 alongside internships, and which ones?

**Answer:** Yes, apply to two: the Example Summer Research Program and the Sample Institute Fellowship. Their deadlines (2099-11-08 and 2100-01-03) come before most internship decisions, and their stipends are close to the internship pay Alex is seeing. Skip the Placeholder Field School unless fieldwork becomes a goal. Part of the run [[research/2099-09-26-summer-research-programs]].

## Findings

### Fit and eligibility

- Both recommended programs accept juniors with one methods course, completed or in progress; SAMPLE 210 counts. [Source: [[research/2099-09-26-summer-research-programs/sweep-programs]]]
- The Sample Institute Fellowship asks for two letters, at least one from faculty, so getting to know a potential PI on [[career/research-lab-prospects]] this fall matters for it.

### Money

- The stipend plus housing roughly matches an hourly internship over 10 weeks. [Source: [[research/2099-09-26-summer-research-programs/sweep-funding]]]

## Where sources disagree or are stale

- The research office list gives the Example Summer Research Program deadline as 2099-10-25; the program's own page says 2099-11-08. Trust the program page, and re-check it in November.

## What would change the answer

- An internship offer with a decision deadline before 2100-01-03.
