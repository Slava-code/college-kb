# DEMO 101 syllabus, fall 2099

## Source

- **What:** course syllabus, 6 pages
- **By:** Prof. Example · **Published:** 2099-08-21 · **Captured:** 2099-08-25
- **Where:** `https://example.edu/demo-101/syllabus` · **Copy on file:** `raw/school/2099-08-25-demo-101-syllabus.pdf` (manifest entry; sha256 recorded there)

## Key claims

- Two lecture sections share homework and exams. (§Course structure, p. 1)
- Grading: homework 25%, discussion quizzes 10%, Midterm 1 20%, Midterm 2 20%, final 25%; the final replaces the lower midterm if higher; cutoffs not curved. (§Grading, p. 2)
- Homework is due Fridays 11:59 p.m. on ExampleHW (publisher site, linked from the LMS); no late work. (§Homework, p. 3)
- AI tools may explain concepts; AI-generated work may not be submitted; any tool used must be named on each homework. (§Academic integrity, p. 5)
- Midterm 1 covers "1.1–2.4"; dates as on the course page. (§Exams, p. 4)

## Caveats

- The final-replaces-lower-midterm rule is not set up in the LMS gradebook, so the LMS total differs from the syllabus grade.
- Midterm 1 scope was later widened to 2.6 by announcement (2099-09-28); the syllabus was not updated.
